CAN A CARGO SHIP BECOME A HOME?

// ABYSSORA //

FONDATION JACQUES ROUGERIE - GRAND PRIX

Each year, over 700 ships are dismantled worldwide, with 70 soon in Oman. ABYSSORA diverts this fate: ships once built to transport goods become the cores of floating cities, surrounded by modules and breathing patches. Inspired by fish scales, these fragments overlap as protective layers, filtering light, producing energy, and sheltering both people and ecosystems. Over time, they grow into an archipelago, a new urbanity born from abandoned wrecks.

This project was awarded the "Grand Prix: Architecture & Innovation for the Sea" by the Fondation Jacques Rougerie and is being published by different magazines. It is an ongoing process with potential for collaboration. If you are interested, reach out.
