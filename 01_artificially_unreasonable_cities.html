CAN ARCHITECTURE MAKE CITIES ARTIFICIALLY UNREASONABLE?

// ARTIFICIALLY UNREASONABLE CITIES //

CONFLUENCE INSTITUTE - DIPLOMA PROJECT

The urban public space is perhaps the most important space of collective political life. It is the breeding ground for negotiation that enables our democracîes. But it is currently being automized by private companies with their own incentives. We all share the responsibility to decide: What do we want our future "smart" cities to be?

The project was developed in dialogue with my dissertation on "Artificially Intelligent Cities". It explores the possibility of designing the public urban space of the smart city around three principles: participation, transparency, and friction.
