CAN ARCHITECTURE BECOME ARTIFICIALLY UNREASONABLE?

// ARTIFICIALLY UNREASONABLE CITIES //

CONFLUENCE INSTITUTE

What happens when architecture resists the hyper-rationalization of algorithmic urbanism? Artificially Unreasonable Cities explores spatial interventions engineered to provoke unpredictability, sensory friction, and human spontaneity.

Through multi-level sectional cuts, acoustic microphone arrays, olfactory channels, and elevated balcony perches, the building creates zones of uncalculated interaction. The structure operates as an anti-machine—disrupting seamless surveillance and inviting raw, unscripted urban life back into the streetscape.
