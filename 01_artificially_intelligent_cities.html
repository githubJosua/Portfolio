Algorithmic Control Negotiated by Human Unreason.

As smart infrastructure and predictive algorithms expand into every layer of urban space, the city is transformed into a continuous real-time feedback loop. Urban dynamics are measured, optimized, and monetary value extracted from human movements.

This dissertation and design investigation proposes friction over optimization. By introducing physical and spatial unreason into the algorithmic street matrix, human agency reclaims the city. Through mapping personal data extractions, predictive loops, and network traffic disruptions, the project outlines a counter-framework to algorithmic determinism.
